Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Got questions about setup, drivers, or network routing? Here are the answers.

Why did Wi-Fi say "No PCs found" or "Reconnection failed" on my home Wi-Fi?
Many home fiber routers (like JioFiber or Airtel) have a security setting enabled by default called AP Isolation (Client Isolation). This feature allows both devices to browse the web, but blocks wireless devices from communicating with each other on the local network. You can bypass this instantly by turning on your phone's Mobile Hotspot, connecting your PC to it, or by plugging in a USB cable!
How do I select Vixxo inside Discord, Zoom, Teams, or OBS Studio?
Vixxo plays audio directly into the virtual input of VB-CABLE. In your voice settings (e.g., Discord Voice & Video settings), simply choose "CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)" as your Input Device (Microphone). Vixxo handles the rest automatically.
Why does Vixxo use VB-CABLE instead of a custom kernel driver?
Custom audio kernel drivers on Windows require EV code-signing certificates and frequently cause Blue Screen of Death (BSOD) crashes during Windows updates or sleep/wake cycles. VB-CABLE is the industry standard for virtual audio routing, used safely by millions of podcasters and gamers worldwide.
How do I enable USB streaming with the lowest latency?
Enable "USB Debugging" in your Android phone's Developer Options, plug in the USB cable, and open Vixxo on PC. Vixxo's automated ADB watcher detects the device and maps port 47800 over the wire. On your phone app, simply tap "USB" and "Start Streaming" for sub-5ms studio audio.
